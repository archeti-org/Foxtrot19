=================================
Foxtrot Product Label Custom
=================================

Adds two custom product label formats to the **Print Labels** wizard:

* **4" x 4"** (101.6 mm × 101.6 mm) — Kanban-style label with default code,
  barcode, product photo, product name, supplier, category and price.
  Sheet layout: **1 column × 2 rows**.
* **4" x 1 3/4"** (101.6 mm × 44.45 mm) — Compact horizontal label with
  default code, product name, barcode, product photo and category.
  Sheet layout: **1 column × 4 rows**.
